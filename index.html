3 · Brand Position Strategic Architecture
Refining PMG’s brand narrative to move away from generic messaging and turn membership into a high-converting retention engine.

SUGGESTION ⚠️

  • Reduce generic messaging like “Affordable, Friendly, Professional, Comprehensive” as the main differentiator.
  • Reduce reliance on “Malaysia’s No. 1” as the hero claim; replace with verifiable operational evidence and clinical impact.
  • Reduce fragmented service-list thinking; stop marketing clinics and pharmacies as standalone entities.

KEEP + REFRAME 🔄

  • Keep core strengths: Community outreach, staff training & development, key wellness initiatives, e-store, and multi-disciplinary ecosystem.
  • Reframe Narrative: Shift from “we have many disconnected services” to “one integrated network following the patient across life stages and care needs.”

REVITALIZE MEMBERSHIP 🚀

Based on an assessment of pmg2u.com/membership, membership should transition from a passive point-accrual card into an active Personal Health Passport.

Key Push Factors to Drive Sign-ups:
  • Instant Tiered Member Discounts across Pharmacy & Clinics
  • Free Annual Health Screening Vouchers upon registration
  • Integrated Digital Prescription & Health Tracker history
  • Exclusive Family Multi-user Pass rewards
Proposed Brand Positioning Tagline: “Connected Care, Wherever Life Happens.” (Strategic direction for executive alignment).
4 · B2B / OHD Growth Project
A pragmatic growth engine unlocking corporate and Occupational Health Doctor (OHD) revenues using existing physical clinic and pharmacy footprints.
STAGE 1

MAP

Identify PMG branches within a 5km radius of industrial parks, factories, logistics hubs, and commercial centers.

STAGE 2

PACKAGE

Structure 3 modular employer tiers: Essential Screening, OHD Regulatory Compliance, and Full Workforce Wellness.

STAGE 3

PILOT

Deploy pilot programs across 10–20 target employers in 2 regions. Offer bundled vaccination, health talks, and GP access.

STAGE 4

SCALE

Roll out enterprise employer dashboard, annual renewal cadences, and automated referral routing to specialty care.

Competitive Logic: Competitors like Careclinics and Mediviron are aggressively targeting Corporate SLAs. PMG can outmaneuver them by bundling Workplace GP + OHD screening + On-site Pharmacy supplies + Ergonomic Physio as a single holistic contract, leveraging our higher Ecosystem Breadth.
5 · PMO 90-Day Execution Plan
Achievable, high-impact milestones designed for realistic PMO capacity, focusing on measurable operational outputs.
DAYS 1 – 30
DIAGNOSE & MAP

Baseline Capacity Audit & Geographic Mapping

Audit high-density PMG branches + newly acquired branches (Target 2 to 3 branches each). Pinpoint patient journey bottlenecks, evaluate current membership sign-up friction, and map local corporate employer targets nearby.

Measurable Output: Branch Operational Diagnostic Report & B2B Target List
DAYS 31 – 60
PILOT & TEST

Proposal Stage & Integration

For newly acquired clinics: Transition & Integration Checklist:

  • Onboarding acquired employees & issuing employee handbooks
  • Standardizing cash collection practices
  • Operational compliance (including facilities reporting and SLAs)
  • Compliance on Working hours completion
  • Corporate/Marketing team need to do panel notification (TPAs and Non-TPAs)

Client Servicing (Integration Stage): Corporate/marketing teams must conduct client servicing from the acquired clinics. This includes informing the panel companies and performing physical visits. Assess OHD needs, pre-employment screening, vaccination, etc., based on the client industry. Pitch potential membership discounts on LTM or other corporate/patient services.

DAYS 61 – 90
STANDARDISE & SCALE

Build Rollout Playbook for Executive

Consolidate existing performance data into a standardized Playbook & SOP. This playbook need to update regularly every month. Launch an optimized Membership Push campaign alongside an initial B2B corporate outreach pilot, especially during transition period.

Measurable Output: Target 10+ Signed Pilot Employer Accounts
